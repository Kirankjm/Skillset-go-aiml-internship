FITNESS GUIDE FOR BEGINNERS

1. BASIC PRINCIPLES
- Start slow and gradually increase intensity
- Consistency is more important than intensity
- Aim for 150 minutes of moderate exercise per week
- Include both cardio and strength training
- Rest days are essential for recovery

2. CARDIO EXERCISES
- Walking: 30 minutes daily
- Running: Start with 1 minute run, 2 minute walk intervals
- Cycling: 20-30 minutes at moderate pace
- Swimming: Full body workout, low impact
- Jump rope: Great for cardiovascular health

3. STRENGTH TRAINING
- Bodyweight exercises: Push-ups, squats, lunges, planks
- Start with 2-3 sets of 8-12 repetitions
- Rest 60-90 seconds between sets
- Train each muscle group 2-3 times per week
- Focus on form before adding weight

4. STRETCHING AND FLEXIBILITY
- Stretch after every workout
- Hold each stretch for 15-30 seconds
- Include dynamic stretching before workouts
- Static stretching after workouts
- Yoga improves flexibility and reduces stress

5. WORKOUT PLANNING
- Plan workouts for the week ahead
- Include variety to prevent boredom
- Track progress in a journal
- Adjust plan based on progress
- Listen to your body and rest when needed

6. COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID
- Skipping warm-up and cool-down
- Lifting too heavy too soon
- Not staying hydrated
- Inconsistent training schedule
- Ignoring pain and injuries
