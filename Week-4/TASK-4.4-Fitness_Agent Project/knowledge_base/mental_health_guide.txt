MENTAL HEALTH GUIDE

1. BASICS OF MENTAL HEALTH
- Mental health is as important as physical health
- It affects how we think, feel, and act
- Mental health can be improved with proper care and attention
- Seeking help is a sign of strength, not weakness
- Everyone experiences mental health challenges differently

2. COMMON MENTAL HEALTH CONDITIONS
- Anxiety: Excessive worry, restlessness, difficulty concentrating
- Depression: Persistent sadness, loss of interest, fatigue
- Stress: Physical and emotional response to pressure
- Burnout: Emotional, physical, and mental exhaustion
- PTSD: Anxiety from traumatic events

3. STRESS MANAGEMENT TECHNIQUES
- Deep breathing exercises (4-7-8 technique)
- Regular physical activity
- Mindfulness and meditation
- Time management and prioritization
- Social support and connection
- Adequate sleep (7-9 hours)
- Limiting caffeine and alcohol

4. MINDFULNESS PRACTICES
- Start with 5-10 minutes daily
- Focus on the present moment
- Observe thoughts without judgment
- Use guided meditation apps
- Practice mindful eating and walking
- Body scan meditation

5. BUILDING RESILIENCE
- Maintain positive relationships
- Accept change as part of life
- Take decisive action when facing problems
- Look for opportunities for self-discovery
- Nurture a positive view of yourself
- Keep things in perspective

6. WHEN TO SEEK PROFESSIONAL HELP
- Persistent feelings of sadness or hopelessness
- Difficulty functioning in daily life
- Withdrawal from social activities
- Changes in sleep or appetite
- Thoughts of self-harm
- Substance abuse
- Extreme mood swings

7. HEALTHY LIFESTYLE FOR MENTAL HEALTH
- Regular exercise (30 minutes daily)
- Balanced diet rich in omega-3 fatty acids
- Adequate sleep and consistent schedule
- Limit screen time and social media
- Practice gratitude daily
- Engage in hobbies and activities
- Connect with nature regularly

8. WORK-LIFE BALANCE
- Set clear boundaries between work and personal time
- Take regular breaks during work
- Learn to say no to additional commitments
- Prioritize self-care activities
- Disconnect from work outside office hours
- Use vacation time for true rest
