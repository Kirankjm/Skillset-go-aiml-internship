HYDRATION HEALTH GUIDE

1. IMPORTANCE OF HYDRATION
- Water is essential for all body functions
- 60% of human body is water
- Proper hydration improves physical performance
- Hydration affects cognitive function
- Water helps regulate body temperature

2. DAILY WATER INTAKE
- General recommendation: 8 glasses (2 liters) per day
- Individual needs vary by weight, activity, climate
- Athletes may need 3-4 liters daily
- Hot weather increases water needs
- Listen to your body's thirst signals

3. SIGNS OF DEHYDRATION
- Dark yellow urine
- Dry mouth and lips
- Fatigue and dizziness
- Headache
- Decreased urine output
- Muscle cramps
- Dry skin

4. HYDRATION FOR EXERCISE
- Drink water before, during, and after exercise
- Pre-workout: 500ml 2 hours before
- During exercise: 150-250ml every 15-20 minutes
- Post-workout: Replace fluids lost through sweat
- For intense exercise >1 hour, consider sports drinks
- Weigh yourself before and after to track fluid loss

5. BEST FLUIDS FOR HYDRATION
- Water (best choice)
- Herbal teas
- Coconut water (natural electrolytes)
- Milk (provides nutrients)
- Fruits and vegetables with high water content
- Avoid excessive caffeine and alcohol

6. HYDRATION MYTHS
- Myth: You must drink 8 glasses exactly
- Reality: Needs vary by individual
- Myth: Coffee dehydrates you
- Reality: Mild diuretic effect, still hydrating
- Myth: Thirst means you're already dehydrated
- Reality: Thirst is a reliable indicator for most people

7. HYDRATION BY ACTIVITY LEVEL
- Sedentary: 2-2.5 liters daily
- Light activity: 2.5-3 liters daily
- Moderate activity: 3-3.5 liters daily
- Heavy exercise: 4+ liters daily
- Hot/humid conditions: Increase intake by 1-2 liters

8. HYDRATION FOR SPECIAL CONDITIONS
- Pregnancy: Extra 300ml daily
- Breastfeeding: Extra 700-1000ml daily
- Illness (fever, vomiting, diarrhea): Increase significantly
- Elderly: May need reminders to drink
- Children: Smaller, more frequent servings
