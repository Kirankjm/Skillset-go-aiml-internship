DISEASE PREVENTION GUIDE

1. BASIC PREVENTION PRINCIPLES
- Prevention is better than cure
- Healthy lifestyle reduces disease risk
- Regular check-ups detect problems early
- Vaccinations prevent many diseases
- Hygiene prevents infection spread

2. CARDIOVASCULAR HEALTH
- Maintain healthy blood pressure (below 120/80)
- Keep cholesterol levels in check
- Don't smoke or use tobacco
- Limit alcohol consumption
- Manage stress effectively
- Regular cardiovascular exercise

3. DIABETES PREVENTION
- Maintain healthy weight
- Regular physical activity
- Balanced diet low in sugar
- Monitor blood sugar levels
- Stay hydrated
- Get enough sleep

4. CANCER PREVENTION
- Avoid tobacco in all forms
- Protect skin from UV radiation
- Get vaccinated (HPV, Hepatitis B)
- Limit processed and red meats
- Maintain healthy weight
- Regular screenings (age-appropriate)

5. INFECTIOUS DISEASE PREVENTION
- Wash hands frequently with soap
- Use hand sanitizer when soap unavailable
- Cover mouth when coughing/sneezing
- Avoid close contact with sick people
- Stay home when ill
- Keep vaccinations up to date

6. IMMUNE SYSTEM SUPPORT
- Eat vitamin-rich foods (C, D, zinc)
- Get adequate sleep
- Manage stress levels
- Exercise regularly
- Stay hydrated
- Avoid excessive alcohol

7. HYGIENE PRACTICES
- Daily showering or bathing
- Regular dental care (brushing, flossing)
- Clean living environment
- Food safety practices
- Proper wound care
- Regular laundry of clothes and bedding

8. SCREENING GUIDELINES
- Blood pressure: Every 2 years (or more often if high)
- Cholesterol: Every 4-6 years
- Diabetes: Every 3 years after age 45
- Colonoscopy: Every 10 years after age 45
- Mammograms: Every 1-2 years after age 40-50
- Pap smears: Every 3 years after age 21
- Prostate screening: Discuss with doctor after age 50

9. WARNING SIGNS TO WATCH
- Unexplained weight loss
- Persistent fatigue
- Changes in bowel or bladder habits
- Unusual bleeding or discharge
- Persistent cough or hoarseness
- Changes in moles or skin growths
- Difficulty swallowing
- Persistent indigestion
