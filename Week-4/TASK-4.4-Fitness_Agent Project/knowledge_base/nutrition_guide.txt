NUTRITION GUIDE FOR BEGINNERS

1. BASIC NUTRITION PRINCIPLES
- Eat a balanced diet with all food groups
- Focus on whole, unprocessed foods
- Stay hydrated with 8-10 glasses of water daily
- Don't skip meals, especially breakfast
- Portion control is key to weight management

2. MACRONUTRIENTS
- Proteins: Build and repair muscle (1.2-2.0g per kg body weight)
  Sources: Chicken, fish, eggs, beans, nuts, tofu
- Carbohydrates: Primary energy source (45-65% of daily calories)
  Sources: Whole grains, fruits, vegetables, legumes
- Fats: Essential for hormone production (20-35% of daily calories)
  Sources: Avocados, nuts, olive oil, fatty fish

3. MICRONUTRIENTS
- Vitamins: Support various body functions
  Vitamin A: Carrots, sweet potatoes, leafy greens
  Vitamin C: Citrus fruits, bell peppers, strawberries
  Vitamin D: Fatty fish, fortified foods, sunlight
  B Vitamins: Whole grains, meat, eggs, legumes
- Minerals: Build bones, regulate fluids
  Calcium: Dairy, leafy greens, fortified foods
  Iron: Red meat, beans, spinach, fortified cereals
  Potassium: Bananas, potatoes, beans

4. MEAL TIMING
- Eat every 3-4 hours to maintain energy
- Pre-workout meal: 1-2 hours before exercise
  Carbohydrates for energy, moderate protein
- Post-workout meal: Within 30-60 minutes after exercise
  Protein for muscle repair, carbs for glycogen replenishment
- Avoid heavy meals right before bed

5. HEALTHY SNACKING
- Choose nutrient-dense snacks
- Examples: Greek yogurt, nuts, fruit, hummus
- Keep portions small (100-200 calories)
- Plan snacks to avoid unhealthy choices

6. SPECIAL DIETARY CONSIDERATIONS
- Vegetarians: Ensure adequate protein from plant sources
- Vegans: Supplement B12, iron, and omega-3s
- Gluten-free: Focus on naturally gluten-free whole foods
- Food allergies: Read labels carefully, have alternatives

7. SAMPLE MEAL CALORIES
- Breakfast: 300-500 calories
- Lunch: 400-600 calories
- Dinner: 400-600 calories
- Snacks: 100-200 calories each
