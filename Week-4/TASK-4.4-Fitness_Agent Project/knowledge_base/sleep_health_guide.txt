SLEEP HEALTH GUIDE

1. IMPORTANCE OF SLEEP
- Sleep is essential for physical and mental health
- Adults need 7-9 hours of sleep per night
- Quality of sleep is as important as quantity
- Sleep affects memory, mood, and immune function
- Chronic sleep deprivation increases health risks

2. SLEEP CYCLES
- Each sleep cycle lasts approximately 90 minutes
- 4 stages: N1, N2, N3 (deep sleep), REM
- Most deep sleep occurs in first half of night
- REM sleep increases in second half of night
- Average adult needs 4-6 complete cycles per night

3. BETTER SLEEP HYGIENE
- Maintain consistent sleep schedule (even weekends)
- Create a relaxing bedtime routine
- Keep bedroom cool, dark, and quiet
- Avoid screens 1 hour before bed
- Limit caffeine after 2 PM
- Avoid large meals before bedtime
- Exercise regularly but not close to bedtime

4. COMMON SLEEP DISORDERS
- Insomnia: Difficulty falling or staying asleep
- Sleep apnea: Breathing stops during sleep
- Restless leg syndrome: Uncomfortable leg sensations
- Narcolepsy: Excessive daytime sleepiness
- Circadian rhythm disorders: Misaligned sleep-wake cycle

5. NATURAL SLEEP AIDS
- Melatonin supplements (short-term use)
- Valerian root
- Chamomile tea
- Lavender aromatherapy
- Magnesium supplements
- Warm bath before bed

6. SLEEP AND PRODUCTIVITY
- Well-rested people are more productive
- Sleep improves problem-solving and creativity
- Lack of sleep impairs decision-making
- Naps can boost alertness (20-30 minutes)
- Power naps shouldn't replace night sleep

7. SLEEP TRACKING
- Use sleep tracking apps or devices
- Monitor sleep duration and quality
- Identify patterns and triggers
- Track factors affecting sleep
- Consult doctor if problems persist

8. SLEEP FOR DIFFERENT AGES
- Newborns (0-3 months): 14-17 hours
- Infants (4-11 months): 12-15 hours
- Toddlers (1-2 years): 11-14 hours
- Preschoolers (3-5 years): 10-13 hours
- School-age (6-13 years): 9-11 hours
- Teenagers (14-17 years): 8-10 hours
- Adults (18-64 years): 7-9 hours
- Older adults (65+): 7-8 hours
